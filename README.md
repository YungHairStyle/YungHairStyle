# Asaad Hanna

**Physics MSc student and teaching assistant at Concordia University, Montreal.**

I work on quantum algorithms and scientific computing, with an interest in using quantum computers to investigate interacting quantum systems and fundamental physics.

## Current research

With **Abhijeet Alase**, I investigate the accuracy and resource costs of quantum algorithms for implementing functions of unitary operators. My interests include Laurent approximation, quantum signal processing, block encodings, and Hamiltonian simulation. I combine mathematical analysis with computational comparisons; bounds and optimality questions are ongoing research directions.

## Quantum simulation experience

During my **2026 AlgoLabs internship at Université de Sherbrooke**, supervised by **Simon Verret**, I extended a Liouvillian-recursion workflow for Green's functions to odd-site Hubbard models with degenerate ground states. Working with the team, I implemented fermionic-swap circuits connecting spin-related ground states and combined pure-state contributions to evaluate the ground-state ensemble without directly preparing a mixed state. The work included exact-diagonalization benchmarks, Qiskit simulations, IBM quantum-hardware runs, and exploration of symmetry-based qubit tapering.
